❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How do I track my delivery in real-time? ▾
Click on "Track My Orders" at any time. You can view real-time updates through Confirmed ➔ Quality Checked ➔ Dispatched via BlueDart Express (with live tracking AWB) ➔ Out for Delivery ➔ Delivered.
What is SHYN's 7-Day Doorstep Return & Exchange Policy? ▾
We provide hassle-free 7-day doorstep returns and exchanges. As long as items are unworn and tags remain intact, our logistics partner will arrange pickup right from your doorstep at zero charge.
Are SHYN sarees certified pure handloom with blouse piece included? ▾
Yes! All sarees are Handloom Certified with authentic zari work. Each saree measures standard 5.5 meters with finished fall & pico edges, plus an attached 0.8 meter matching unstitched blouse piece.
What payment methods are supported? ▾
We support Cash on Delivery (COD) with free shipping on orders above ₹1,000, as well as Instant Online Payment via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards, and Net Banking.

  • Saree Width: 44–45 Inches (Standard universal drape height).
  • Blouse Fabric: 0.8 Meter unstitched matching piece included at the inner end.
  • Edges & Fall: Pre-finished with traditional fall and pico hem for ready drape.
